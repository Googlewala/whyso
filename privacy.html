[COMPANY NAME] ("we") runs [APP NAME]. This policy explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

On your phone: the entries, people, plans and budgets you add. They stay on your device unless you sign in. If you sign in with Google: your email address and the data you choose to sync, stored on Cloudflare servers. When you subscribe: Razorpay processes the payment and shares your name, email and payment status with us. We never see or store your card details, UPI PIN or bank login.

What we do not do

No ads, no third-party advertising trackers, and we never sell your data.

Why we use it

To run the app, sync your data, check your plan, prevent abuse and answer support requests.

Your rights

You can export your data (CSV or backup), delete all data on your phone, and delete your account and cloud data inside the app (Me tab). You can withdraw consent at any time. You can also email us to access or correct your data.

Retention and security

Cloud data is kept until you delete your account. We use HTTPS and access controls. No system is perfectly secure.
